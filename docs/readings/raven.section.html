What the Raven knows

Odin kept two ravens. Huginn means thought. Muninn is usually read as memory1. Each day they flew the world and came back to whisper what they saw1. Odin feared for Thought, and more for Memory1. You were born with both birds.

Four parts, one bird. Start anywhere.

Huginn flies first.

Your mind

Aquarius is fixed Air, ruled by Uranus and, in the older books, by Saturn2. The sign is inventive and at ease being different2. The Monkey adds yang Metal3. Witty and quick to learn4. So you stand at the edge of the group and see how it fits. Then you do the clever thing nobody saw coming.

The raven does it too

Ravens plan. In a 2017 study they chose a tool or a token and saved it for later use5. Ravens also play, and Heinrich and Smolker reported them sliding down snowy roofs on their backs6. Same bird. Same mind. You can plan ten moves out and still want the roof.

Muninn comes home heavier.

What you hide

A raven that sees a rival watching will cache its food farther off, or wait7. It also treats a rival who saw the hiding differently from one who missed it8. You keep a second place for what matters. Few people know where it is.

What you file

In a 2017 study, ravens still chose a fair trader over a cheat a month later9,10. Your file on who played you is short. It is accurate. You open it far less often than they fear.

The few you let watch you cache.

Love comes late, then stays. The Monkey is slow to settle and fully committed once it chooses4. Ravens pair for life11. You test someone for a long time and never call it a test. Pass, and you have them.
