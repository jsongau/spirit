Zodi Animal reading · Libra born in a Goat year

What the Luna Moth means

In the Book of Songs, a poet praising a great beauty gave her a cicada's forehead and a moth's eyebrows, 螓首蛾眉 (qín shǒu é méi)1. The moth's feathered antennae were the loveliest brow the poet knew1. In Chinese, the moth begins as beauty. You were born under that brow, a Libra ruled by Venus2 in the gentle Goat year3.

Three parts, best read after dark.

Venus Air above, soft Earth below.

Your temperament

Libra is cardinal Air, the sign of the scales2. The Goat is yin Earth, gentle and artistic, and slow to change3,4. In you the scales hang over a soft heart. Once the weighing is done, you settle and give.

The luna moth does it too

A male luna moth can follow a female's scent from several miles away5. As an adult it cannot feed, and it lives about a week5. It has nothing to waste on the wrong signal. Neither does your taste, the brightest thing about you.

The old warning, and the luck inside it.

Your shadow

Libra's old weakness is indecision2. The Goat clings once it lands3. So you hover at the edge of a choice for weeks. Then the waiting gets lonely and you dive at whatever glows closest.

When you quarrel

Your fights happen at night. By day you smooth things over, and the argument grows in the dark. The scales still want a fair hearing2. One calm talk, both sides weighed, puts the flame back in its lamp.

Three lamps worth flying to, one to pass by.

In love you fall through atmosphere: a voice, a scent, a table set with care. The nearest light is always there, and it is rarely the one. Three old pairings favor you.

The old warningChinese keeps a four-character warning for the moth that throws itself into the fire, 飞蛾扑火 (fēi é pū huǒ). It means rushing toward your own ruin, and the novel Water Margin uses it7.

The pull that could take you into the flame is the same one that finds the right lamp across miles of dark.
