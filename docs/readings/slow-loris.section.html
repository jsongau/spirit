Zodi Animal reading · Scorpio born in a Snake year

What the Slow Loris guards

In Borneo, people told researchers that slow lorises kept the gate of heaven1. Each person had a loris of their own, waiting there after death1. Picture it. A small animal with enormous eyes. A threshold in the dark. No hurry at all. The rest of this reading is about what you guard.

Four parts. Take them slowly.

Deep water, low fire.

Your temperament

Scorpio is fixed Water, ruled by Mars in the old books and Pluto in the new2. The Snake is yin Fire, calm and observant, a deep thinker3. Both halves are yin. So your heat stays under the surface, and few people ever see it rise.

The loris does it too

The slow loris moves slowly along the vines and does not leap4. Researchers think it may mimic the spectacled cobra, its hunched arms working like a hood5. A Snake-year animal in a snake's shape. You also carry more warning than your size suggests.

What you hold back is the point.

Your mending

Hunters in Mondulkiri, Cambodia, said a loris that fell could heal its own broken bones at once1. You keep that kind of reserve. You fall, you set the bone yourself, and others hear about it later, if at all.

Your shadow

The Snake's old weaknesses are suspicion and possessiveness3. Left alone, the guard stays on duty long after the danger has gone home.

Three may come in. One waits outside.

You watch a person for a long time from the far branch. You need someone who will not rush you or laugh at the shyness.
