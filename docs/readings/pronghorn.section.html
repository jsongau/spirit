Zodi Animal reading · Aries born in a Horse year

What the Pronghorn runs on

Mars rules Aries1. In Chinese, Mars is 火星 (huǒxīng), the Fire Star2. Its old name, 荧惑 (yínghuò), was read as an omen of disorder2. Your year animal burns too. The Horse is yang Fire, the branch of noon3,4. Fire on fire. No water between. You came in at a run.

Three parts. Go fast.

First to move, last to tire.

Your temperament

Aries is cardinal Fire, the first sign, the pioneer1,5. The Horse is lively and perceptive, and bad at hiding what it feels6. One starts. The other keeps going. In you that makes a fast start and a long engine. Your face breaks the news first.

The rush

The old books call Aries brave and forthright, and also rash and impatient1. The Horse's listed flaw is impulse6. So you leap first. Mostly you land well. When you don't, you left before the ground was clear.

Three who run with you. One who trips you.

In love you fall fast and need space. You show care by doing things side by side at speed, and a road trip says it better than a card. Three old pairings run your way.

Pick a long road. Then take it.

At work

Emergency calls and breaking news both pay for the first move. Travel China Guide lists adventure and tour work among the Horse's trades6.

An old road

Pronghorn have used one route between Grand Teton and the Upper Green River for at least 6,000 years9. In 2008 it became the first federally designated wildlife migration corridor in the US9. Speed is half your story. The other half is a road you keep coming back to.

An Arapaho charmThe Arapaho word for the pronghorn is nisíce10. Children wore amulets of antelope horn so they would grow and walk quickly, and the same kind of charm was used to make horses run faster10.
