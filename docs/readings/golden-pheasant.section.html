Zodi Animal reading · Leo born in a Rabbit year

What the Golden Pheasant wears

Under the Ming and Qing, a civil official of the second rank wore a golden pheasant, 錦雞 (jǐnjī), sewn on the chest and back of his robe1. Only the crane ranked above it1. The bird's name sounded like a word for ruling, and writers tied it to literary talent and military prowess1. That badge fits you. You rule by learning, and you dress for the part.

Three parts, in order of rank.

Fire that wants the hall. Wood that keeps its counsel.

Your temperament

Leo is fixed Fire, ruled by the Sun2,3. The Rabbit is yin Wood, elegant and alert, and slow to reveal its mind4,5. In you the caution feeds the brilliance. You enter the hall in full colour and say very little. When you do speak, the court writes it down.

The brocade

The bird's Chinese name means brocade fowl6. The Rabbit's old gifts are painting, calligraphy, tailoring and cooking5. Your taste is a trade, and people trust it before you explain it.

Influence by counsel. Love by ceremony.

Where you serve

The badge belonged to a civil official, not a general1. You do your finest work as counsel beside the throne, as an editor or a chief of staff.

How you court

Leo takes great pride in the relationship7. So you court with ceremony. The table is set before anyone arrives. Admiration given in private holds you longer than any public praise.

Three worth the robe, and one who argues with it.

You dress for very few people. The old pairings say who has earned the full plumage.

An emperor's paintingEmperor Huizong painted a golden pheasant on a bending hibiscus branch8. The scroll hangs in the Palace Museum, Beijing9. His poem says the tall-crowned bird already holds all five virtues8. The old list reads the crown as culture, 文 (wén), and the spurs as martial skill, 武 (wǔ)10. Facing an enemy is courage, 勇 (yǒng)10. Calling others to food is benevolence, 仁 (rén), and keeping the night watch on time is trust, 信 (xìn)10. Benevolence is also the virtue of Wood, your element11.

Wear the robe. The rank was yours before anyone sewed it on.
