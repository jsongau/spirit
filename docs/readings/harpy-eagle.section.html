Zodi Animal reading · Leo born in a Tiger year

What the Harpy Eagle rules

The bird that carries your crossing was named for the harpies, the wind spirits of Greek myth who bore the dead away to Hades1. It is a name for something that owns the air above the canopy. You were born to Leo, the one sign ruled by the Sun, in a year of the Tiger, whose element is yang Wood2,3. We read that Wood as fuel laid under the Fire, so the quieter half of you is always keeping the brighter half alight.

Three parts. Take them in order, from the top of the tree down.

Fire that wants to be seen. Wood that waits to strike.

Your temperament

Leo is fixed Fire, read as warm, proud and generous, with a real need to be recognised2,4. The Tiger is brave, confident and a natural leader, and its shadow is the sudden, impetuous move3,5. In you they meet as a great presence held in reserve, carried at full weight and spent rarely, so that when you finally move, everything below the canopy feels it.

The harpy does it too

A harpy eagle can reportedly hold one perch for up to 23 hours while it scans the trees for sloths and monkeys6. Its talons are the largest of any living eagle, the longest close to 12.5 centimetres1,6. All of that strength sits still for most of the day. Yours does too, and the stillness is where you keep it.

One tree, held for years.

Home

A harpy pair builds a nest about a metre and a half across and may use it over several years1,6. They lay up to two eggs, raise one chick, and breed only every two or three years6. In many South American cultures it is bad luck to cut down the kapok, a tree harpies favour for nesting1. You build the same way, choosing one house or one venture and adding to it season after season, and you take any threat to it personally.

At work

Lead something small and own it outright. Surgery and flying both reward the long watch and the single exact move.

Three who can share the canopy, and one who will contest it.

In love you protect and provide long before you talk. Harpy pairs hold a range of some 30 square kilometres and are believed to stay together for life6,1. The old Chinese pairings name three who could rule beside you.
