What the Koala means

You were born under two signs that both look toward the Moon. Taurus is ruled by Venus, and the Moon is exalted there1. The Rabbit of the Chinese zodiac lives on the Moon as the Jade Rabbit, keeping Chang'e company and pounding the elixir of long life2. So you came in by soft light, and you have always done your best living slowly.

Earth that settles, Wood that keeps watch.

Your temperament

Taurus is fixed Earth, the sign that settles on the one good thing and stays1. The Rabbit is yin Wood, gentle and alert, the animal that keeps watching while it rests3,4. In you the two become a settled watcher. You can sit in one place all afternoon and still know which way you would leave.

The koala does it too

The koala lives the same way. Even among trees of one species, it feeds less on leaves heavy with a toxic compound called sideroxylonal, and it passes over the poorest trees5,6. It chooses by taste. So do you, and once a tree has fed you, a whole forest of better offers has trouble moving you.

Three trees that will feed you, and one that won't.

In love you open late and close slowly. You need someone who reads your stillness as care. The old Chinese pairings give you three easy matches.
