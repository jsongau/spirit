Zodi Animal reading · Cancer born in a Pig year

What the Capybara offers

Your animal's scientific name, Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris, reads as water hog, then water and enjoyment1. You were born under Cancer, the first water sign, in the year of the Pig, whose element is Water as well2,3. So the water runs through you twice, and the name adds that it is meant to be enjoyed. Think of a capybara in a Japanese hot bath in midwinter, eyes half closed, in no hurry at all4. That is how you look when life is going right.

Three parts. Take them slowly, in any order.

Water that holds, Water that shares.

Your temperament

Cancer is cardinal Water, ruled by the Moon, and the old books give it the chest and the stomach, the body's places for feeding and holding2. The Pig is yin Water, read as generous, compassionate and calm under pressure3,5. In you the two make a slow, warm current. You start things, and then you stay with them long enough to make them comfortable.

The capybara does it too

A capybara's eyes, ears and nostrils sit high on its head, so it can lie almost under and still keep track of the bank1. When danger comes, the group heads for the water and waits there6,7. You keep the same habit. Under strain you go under with your senses up, and you surface when the noise has passed.

Feed people. Let someone pay you for it.

Plenty

Jupiter is exalted in Cancer, and the pig is an old symbol of wealth2,8. Your kind of plenty gets shared at a long table. Keep one pot of money nobody else can ask for, because the Pig is said to trust so readily it can be fooled3,5.

At work

Hosting, nursing and early-years teaching all reward the warmth you would give away anyway. Take the job where that warmth has a title and a wage.

Three to share the warm water with, and one to watch from the bank.

In love you are slow to anger and slower to forget. Birds land on capybaras to pick at ticks, and turtles climb aboard9,10. You let people settle on you in much the same way. The old Chinese pairings name three who give the warmth back.

The winter bathIn 1982 a keeper at Izu Shaboten Zoo saw the capybaras gathered in a warm puddle and filled a pool with hot water for them4. The winter bath became a national ritual, with yuzu and rose baths in rotation4,1.

The bath is already warm. Come in, and keep a place free beside you.
