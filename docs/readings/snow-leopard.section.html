What the Snow Leopard means

A monk once painted Milarepa, the eleventh-century yogi, as a snow leopard leading his disciples1. In Bhutan many tales say great lamas crossed between Tibet and Bhutan in that shape1. The body changes. The mind stays. You know something about that.

Three parts. Take your time.

Water and Earth, both facing inward.

Your temperament

Scorpio is fixed Water, a feminine sign2. The Goat is yin Earth3. The Chinese guides call the Goat gentle and private, with few close friends4,5. Scorpio adds depth, and it is slow to forgive2,6. So you live high, apart, and hard to move.

The snow leopard does it too

The snow leopard lives mostly on wild goats and sheep, the ibex, argali, blue sheep and markhor7. A Goat-year animal that hunts goats. You know the gentle ones from the inside. That is why you read them before they speak.

Its other name

In Ladakh the cat is called jatpo, the one who stalks8. Nobody hears you coming either. Most days you are only coming to sit nearby.

Long distance. Then a short, close season.

Your love

Wild snow leopard pairs travel together for one to eight days, mostly within ten meters of each other9. That is your courtship. Mating pairs have even been recorded hunting together7. Once you share the work of living with someone, you have already chosen.

Your work

Give yourself hard ground and a long hold. Audit and fieldwork pay for patience that proves itself later. In Bhutan villagers call the snow leopard a fence1. You guard a small territory, and you guard it well.

Three who make the climb, one who pulls you down.

You do not go looking. The right ones climb up and sit down without asking for anything. The old Chinese pairings name three who can.
