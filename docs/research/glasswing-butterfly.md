## The Glasswing Butterfly as a symbol
- **Spanish-speaking Central and South America.** The glasswing is called espejitos, 'little mirrors' (Henderson 2002, via Wikipedia). The one item here that is about this species itself. [s5]
- **Chinese classical (Zhuangzi, ch. 2).** 莊周夢蝶 Zhuāng Zhōu mèng dié: Zhuang Zhou dreams he is a butterfly, wakes, and cannot tell whether he dreamed the butterfly or the butterfly dreams him; the chapter calls it 物化 wù huà, the changing of things. Butterflies generally, not glasswings. [s10]
- **Chinese decorative rebus.** 蝶 dié sounds like 耋 dié, a word for someone over seventy or eighty, so butterflies wish long life; paired with a cat (貓 māo, like 耄 mào) they make 耄耋 mào dié, a longevity pun. Butterflies also stand for married happiness and lasting love (the Butterfly Lovers). [s11] [s12]
- **Ancient Greek.** ψυχή psūkhḗ means soul, and rarely butterfly: Aristotle, History of Animals 5.19, 'those which one calls psychai' come from caterpillars. The soul and the butterfly share one word. [s13]
- **Aztec (Mesoamerica, the glasswing's range).** Itzpapalotl, 'obsidian butterfly' or 'clawed butterfly', a skeletal warrior goddess who rules Tamoanchan; associated with the moth Rothschildia orizaba. A fierce butterfly figure, not a glasswing one: use only as 'butterflies in Mesoamerican myth', never as glasswing lore. [s14]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** 'Glasswing spirit animal means transparency and honesty' appears on blogs only. The claim that Aztecs saw butterflies as souls of fallen warriors was not supported by the source opened (Wikipedia Itzpapalotl does not say it). (no source)

## Strongest new material
- 綿裡藏針 mián lǐ cáng zhēn, the page's own Goat proverb, is real (s15) and matches both the Goat's 'tough on the inside' (s4) and the toxic-but-clear butterfly.
- Zhuangzi's butterfly dream, 物化 wù huà, from the primary text (s10): the best classical anchor for a metamorphosis animal.
- Greek ψυχή meaning both soul and butterfly in Aristotle (s13), and the espejitos 'little mirrors' name (s5).
- Caution: every non-espejitos item is butterfly lore in general. Say so in the copy.

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: yin Earth (Goat) under cardinal Water (Cancer). Cancer wants a shell; the Goat wants a quiet pasture and no argument. The Glasswing is the animal that gave up the shell and kept the protection somewhere else: open to look at, bitter to bite. Both halves are read as soft outside and tough inside (s2, s4), which the Goat proverb 綿裡藏針 mián lǐ cáng zhēn (a needle hidden in silk floss) states exactly.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: lets people see in without feeling exposed, reads the shade and the light of a place, builds anchors before any big change, carries more than it looks able to.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: so easy to look through that people stop looking for you. Cestrum-or-nothing pickiness turned on people. Goat stubbornness plus Cancer grudge-memory: rarely fights, rarely forgets.
- **Love.** Our reading: Moon-ruled, with a yin year animal. Courtship by preparation, not pursuit (the lek). Needs one specific kind of care and will not thrive on a substitute, however kindly offered. Loyal for life once the silk pad is spun.
- **Money.** Our reading: Earth saver with a Cancer instinct to stock the home; prefers secured, anchored positions to speculative ones. Risk: holding on to a familiar arrangement long after it stopped feeding you.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: goes still and transparent, hoping trouble looks past. When cornered the bitterness shows. Practice: name the boundary out loud instead of relying on being unpalatable.

## Careers (our reading)
- **Nursing, midwifery, palliative and home care.** Our reading: Cancer's caretaking plus the Goat's gentleness; calm presence in hard rooms of a life.
- **Translation, interpreting, editing.** Our reading: the window role: let meaning pass through cleanly with your own mark kept underneath.
- **Optics, materials science, design of glass and light.** Our reading: the nanopillar wing (s6) as a calling card; Earth patience for fine work.
- **Perfumery, botany, horticulture.** Our reading: the male's pheromone made from plant chemistry (s5) and the caterpillar's one host plant.
- **Counselling, social work, child protection.** Our reading: Cancer's 'claws come out when protecting the vulnerable' (s2) inside a soft-looking practice.

## Candidate lines (draft, second person)
1. In the shade of the understory you let the light pass straight through your wings, and the bird hunting for color looks right past you.
2. You open your whole surface to the forest and keep the bitter part where it has always been kept, in the blood.
3. Close enough is a different plant to you; the caterpillar you were would sooner fail on the first leaf than live on a substitute.
4. Before any change you spin the small silk pad first and hook in, and only then let the old skin split.
5. In the lek you wait in the shade and let the scent you made do the courting; chasing was never how you meant to be chosen.
6. Some days you are more mirror than window, and the one beside you sees their own face in your wing before they see you.

Say: 'in tests with related clearwings, the more transparent species were spotted less'. | [s7] |
