## The Andean Condor as a symbol
- **Andean (pre-Columbian).** The condor was associated with the sun deity and believed to rule the upper world. ADW adds that in Inca culture it stands for the heavens, one of the three realms of existence. This lines up neatly with Capricorn's mountain and Saturn's height. [s7] [s8]
- **Peru, Yawar Fiesta (blood festival).** In some Andean bullfights a condor is tied to the bull's back; the condor generally survives and is set free. Arguedas's 1941 novel Yawar Fiesta does not include the condor detail, though it is now the best-known image of the festival. The common reading (condor as Andean, bull as Spanish) did not appear in the sources I opened: unsourced, do not use that gloss. [s7] [s9]
- **Andean nations.** National bird of Bolivia, Chile, Colombia and Ecuador, and a national symbol in Argentina and Peru. Daniel Alomia Robles's zarzuela El Condor Pasa (1913) carries the bird into world music. [s7]
- **Chinese (Rooster half).** Han Ying's Hanshi Waizhuan (Han dynasty) gives the rooster five virtues: civility 文 wén (its comb), martial skill 武 wǔ (its spurs), courage 勇 yǒng (it fights when faced with an enemy), benevolence 仁 rén (it calls others when it finds food) and trustworthiness 信 xìn (it keeps time without fail). The benevolence virtue rhymes with the condor opening a carcass that smaller birds then feed on. [s6] [s8]
- **Chinese (Rooster half).** The Rooster governs 17:00 to 19:00, and folklore says the Jade Emperor set a red flower on its head after it lost the zodiac contest, which is why roosters wear a comb. A red head links the two halves: the condor's bare head flushes with mood. [s4] [s7]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** The 'Eagle and the Condor prophecy' (North and South uniting) is widely repeated on spiritual sites; I found no traditional source and did not use it. Temple of the Condor at Machu Picchu: real site, but I could not open a reliable page describing it. 

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Earth (Capricorn) meets yin Metal (Rooster). Capricorn climbs slowly toward a long goal; the Rooster keeps the clock and wants to be seen exactly as it is. The Condor is the patient sovereign of the upper air: it waits for real lift, then shows up at full size with its feelings written on its face. In Wu Xing, Earth feeds Metal, so the page's 'Capricorn's Earth feeds your Metal' is a fair bridge, but it mixes the Western element with the Chinese phase and should be labelled as our reading.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: covering huge ground on little effort, reading the whole situation before committing, steadiness that lasts decades, a refusal to fake the summit, a habit of opening the hard thing so others can eat too (the Rooster's benevolence).
- **Shadow.** Our reading: arriving after the moment that needed you; Saturn's coldness plus the Rooster's critical streak can read as judgment; cutting people off when overwhelmed; vanity about doing it the hard, correct way.
- **Love.** Our reading: slow to choose, then for life (condors are thought to mate for life). Expects the partner to read the flush rather than hear the speech. Needs someone who does not take the long circling as coldness.
- **Money.** Our reading: frugal and long-range (Capricorn) with Rooster meticulousness; spends on what lasts, keeps one base. Risk: waiting too long for the perfect thermal on an investment or job change.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: gains altitude and goes silent, then lands with a fully formed verdict, often blunt (Rooster frankness). Practice: say you are circling before you vanish.

## Careers
- **Long-horizon strategy, infrastructure, urban or land planning.** Our reading: Saturn's long view and the condor's range; work that pays off after years.
- **Search and rescue, mountain guiding, wildlife or park management.** Our reading: the habitat itself; scanning big terrain from height.
- **Turnaround consulting, crisis repair, restructuring.** Our reading: arrives once the thing is located and opens what others could not (s8: condors usually arrive last and open the carcass).
- **Audit, compliance, quality assurance, editing.** Our reading: the Rooster wins by meticulousness (s4) and Capricorn will not fake a result.
- **Senior leadership in old institutions.** Our reading: Capricorn's drive toward legacy plus the Rooster's sense of timing and presence.
