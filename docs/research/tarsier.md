## The Tarsier as a symbol
- **Malay and Iban (Borneo).** In Malay the tarsier is called kera hantu, 'ghost monkey'; the Iban name is Ingkat. Source is a Borneo history essay based on Carsten Niemitz's 1972 Sarawak Museum Journal piece. The name fits the animal's silent, sudden presence at night. Use as a naming fact, not as a belief system. [t14]
- **Philippines (community not named in source).** Tarsiers are said to be pets of spirits living in balete (strangler fig) trees; anyone who harms one must apologize to the forest spirits or face sickness or hardship. Source is a documentary summary that does not name the community, so attribute it as 'a belief reported in the Philippines'. [t15]
- **Philippines (Sarangani, Mindanao).** Some Indigenous groups leave tarsiers alone in the wild for fear they bring bad luck, which Wikipedia says has helped the species survive there. Local Visayan names include mawumag and mamag; Waray magô. [t8]
- **Chinese naming.** Chinese calls the tarsier 眼鏡猴 yǎnjìnghóu, 'spectacled monkey', for its huge eyes; the alternate name 跗猴 fùhóu, 'tarsus monkey', mirrors the Latin Tarsius (named for the long ankle bones). [t16] [t7]
- **Iban (Borneo), secondary only.** Facts and Details says the Iban once treated the tarsier as a head-hunting omen, a full head turn warning that a head would be lost, and that Dayak call tarsiers hantu (spirits). No primary ethnography located. Weak: do not use without a better source. [t13]
- **Generic 'tarsier spirit animal' meanings (intuition, seeing the unseen).** Unsourced, do not use. Only totem blogs found. (no source)

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: water on water. Fixed Water (Scorpio) is the deep still pool; yang Water (Rat) is the moving midnight river. Both halves are night creatures: Scorpio works under the surface and the Rat owns the 23:00 to 01:00 double hour, which is exactly when a tarsier is awake and hunting. The Tarsier is the watcher who stores every detail (Rat) and holds it until the moment is right (Scorpio).
- **Gifts.** Our reading: extreme attention, the way an eye the size of a brain takes in more than it says; timing; a private signal channel for the few who are tuned in (the 70 kHz call); total commitment once something is chosen (the only fully carnivorous primate).
- **Shadow.** Our reading: Scorpio suspicion plus the Rat's caution can freeze into watching forever. Because the eyes cannot move, every look costs a whole-body turn, so this crossing may avoid looking at what it fears. Stress in the wrong habitat is real: tarsiers do badly in captivity and under flash and handling (t7, t8). Read as: the wrong environment drains you fast.
- **Love.** Our reading: two Water signs make love deep, slow to show and hard to fake. Chooses one person and turns fully toward them. Needs a partner who can hear the quiet frequency and does not demand loud proof. Jealousy is the risk.
- **Money.** Our reading: the Rat saves and spots the angle; Scorpio keeps it private. A careful, research-led investor who stores reserves no one knows about. Risk: hoarding information or money past the point of use.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: goes still, reads the whole branch, then leaps once and lands. Rarely argues in public. Holds grudges (Scorpio) and remembers every detail (Rat), so repair has to be explicit.
