## The Fossa as a symbol
- **Malagasy (general).** The fossa is generally feared by Malagasy people and is often protected by fady (ancestral taboo). It is said to take little children who walk alone into the forest, and some people will not eat it for fear its undesirable qualities pass to whoever eats it. Present as recorded belief; the species is IUCN Vulnerable. [s5]
- **Malagasy, Bevoahazo valley near Ranomafana.** A local fady protects the fossa because it is believed to eat the bodies of ancestors buried in the forest (2008 study reported by Mongabay). Fady apply only to certain species in certain regions, so this is local, not island-wide. [s6]
- **Malagasy folk naming.** The English name comes from Malagasy fosa. Local people distinguish a large fosa mainty, black fossa, and a smaller fosa mena, reddish fossa. [s5]
- **Chinese naming.** Chinese has no traditional lore for the fossa. Its standard names are 馬島長尾狸貓 mǎdǎo chángwěi límāo (Madagascar long-tailed civet-cat) and 馬島獴 mǎdǎo měng (Madagascar mongoose); also 隱肛狸 yǐngāng lí, which translates the genus name Cryptoprocta, hidden anus. [s7]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** Fossa 'spirit animal' or 'totem' meanings on blogs (stealth, independence) have no traditional source located. The Madagascar film franchise is pop culture, not tradition. (no source)

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Fire (Aries, Mars) over yang Water (Rat). Aries moves first; the Rat reads the ground before it moves. In the fossa that becomes a hunter that scouts a huge range alone, then commits all at once. Water quiets Fire, so the nerve comes out cold and measured rather than loud.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: going straight at the hard target (prey close to its own weight), holding wide ground without constant fights (scent borders that work at a distance), reading a situation from above before descending into it head first.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: the range that keeps everyone at the scent line. Never sleeping in the same place twice can look like freedom and work like avoidance. The Rat's quick exits plus Aries impatience can end bonds before they deepen.
- **Love.** Our reading: rare and decisive. The fossa mating season happens at one traditional tree, at nearly the same date each year, and the female chooses. This crossing opens fully to a few people, on its own calendar, and wants a partner who does not crowd the range the rest of the year.
- **Money.** Our reading: Rat thrift with Aries daring. Scouts for a long time, then makes one large, confident move; can hold a position long after it should have been reviewed.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: prefers borders that are read, not enforced. Marks the line early and clearly. Once a fight is actually on, commits fully and does not back down the trunk.

## Fact audit of the live page
- **OK**: Semi-retractable claws and very flexible ankles let the fossa climb down head first and leap between trees.
  Wikipedia: flexible ankles allow it to climb up and down trees head-first; semi-retractable claws. The 'rotate to grip from any angle' wording is a paraphrase; fine. [s5]
- **WRONG**: Genetic studies place the fossa in Eupleridae, 'the family most closely related to Madagascar's mongooses', not true cats; resemblance is convergent.
  Garbled. Eupleridae contains Madagascar's mongooses (Galidia and relatives are in the fossa's own family). Its closest relatives are the African and Asian mongooses, family Herpestidae. Fix: 'in Eupleridae, the family of Madagascar's carnivores, whose closest relatives are the mongooses of Africa and Asia.' Convergence and the small-cougar comparison are OK. [s5]
- **WRONG**: Males hold territories up to 26 km2, 'over twice' the female's; scent-mark year-round from glands near the tail and chest.
  Minor. 26 km2 vs 13 km2 for females is twice, not over twice; and Wikipedia calls them home ranges, not defended territories. Glands are in the anal region and on the chest; year-round marking on rocks, trees and ground is OK. [s5]
- **OK**: Mating at traditional trees reused year after year on almost the same date, on a horizontal limb about 20 m up; up to eight males; the female chooses and changes partners over several days.
  All supported: same tree year after year with remarkable precision as to date; horizontal limbs about 20 m; as many as eight males; female seems to choose, may mate with several males, occupies the tree up to a week. [s5]
- **OK**: Only predator able to take adults of every lemur species; some prey close to 90 percent of its mass; seen hunting in pairs in breeding season, driving prey to a partner on the ground.
  Wikipedia: only predator capable of preying upon adults of all extant lemur species; largest up to 90 percent of fossa weight; in breeding season hunting parties, pairs or mothers and young, one chases lemurs through trees forcing them down to the other. [s5]
- **OK**: Rarely sleeps in the same spot twice; only a mother with cubs returns reliably to one den.
  Wikipedia: generally does not reuse sleeping sites, but females with young return to the same den. [s5]
- **WRONG**: Header hanzi 獴 (rail__han and serial-han), shown with no pinyin.
  獴 měng means mongoose in general; the fossa is not a mongoose (it is a euplerid). Its Chinese names are 馬島長尾狸貓 mǎdǎo chángwěi límāo or the shorter 馬島獴 mǎdǎo měng, Madagascar mongoose. Use 馬島獴 mǎdǎo měng, with pinyin shown. [s7]
- **WRONG**: Purple Star section: 'read for the Aries Rat crossing it keeps landing on' Qi Sha 七殺 qī shā and Wu Qu 武曲 wǔ qū.
  Zi Wei Dou Shu stars are placed from the lunar birth date and hour, not from sun sign plus year animal. No traditional basis for this pairing. Cut, or relabel plainly as our own metaphor (per REVIEW-mojo section 0.3). [s8]
