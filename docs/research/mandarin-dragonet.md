## The Mandarin Dragonet as a symbol
- **English trade name and Chinese alias.** The name mandarinfish refers to its colours, said to recall the robes of an Imperial Chinese mandarin. One Chinese alias says the same thing outright: 官服魚 guānfú yú, 'official-robe fish'. Aquarium-trade aliases include 七彩麒麟 qīcǎi qílín ('seven-colour qilin') and 五彩青蛙 wǔcǎi qīngwā ('five-colour frog'). The formal Chinese name is 花斑連鰭䲗 huābān liánqí xián. A court robe on a thumb-sized fish is a clean bridge to the Rooster's first virtue, civility, worn as the comb. [m7] [m8] [m6] [m18]
- **Science (the strongest bridge).** The mandarinfish and its close relative the psychedelic mandarin are the only two vertebrates known to make blue from a cellular pigment; the cells were named cyanophores (Goda and Fujii 1995). Nearly every other animal blue is structural, made by light scattering off crystals. Taiwan's National Museum of Natural Science notes the blue organelles gather and disperse inside the cell, shifting how deep the blue looks. We could not open the 1995 paper itself (bot wall), so cite it via Wikipedia or NMNS. [m7] [m10] [m9]
- **Science, behaviour.** Pairs spawn at sunset at the same sites night after night. In a Palau field study, all 198 timed spawns fell between 16 minutes before and 18 minutes after official sunset; the pair rises slowly (7 to 12 seconds) about one metre, releases eggs and sperm, and drops back. A Rooster keeps time without fail; this fish keeps sunset. [m11] [m14] [m6]
- **Science, defence.** No scales. The skin carries a thick mucus coat with an unpleasant smell, and a second cell type secretes toxins thought to repel predators. Wikipedia is more cautious ('debatably smelly and bitter slime'), so phrase it as reported. [m7] [m12] [m13]
- **Classical Chinese (already on the page).** Xunzi, 勸學 Quàn Xué: 青，取之於藍，而青於藍 qīng, qǔ zhī yú lán, ér qīng yú lán, 'blue is taken from the indigo plant yet is bluer than the plant'; the source of the idiom 青出於藍而勝於藍 qīng chū yú lán ér shèng yú lán. 藍 here is the dye plant 蓼藍 liǎolán. The page ties it to the cyanophore blue and labels the pairing as its own; that framing is honest and the source checks out. [m15]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** No myth, totem or spirit-animal tradition about the mandarinfish itself was found in any non-blog source. Generic 'mandarin fish spirit animal' meanings online are invented. Mandarin duck 鴛鴦 yuānyāng fidelity lore belongs to the duck, not this fish. [m16]

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: yin Metal feeding mutable Water. The Rooster half wants exactness, a schedule and a visible standard; the Pisces half moves by feel and shifts with the current. In this fish the two meet as a creature that looks like pure display yet runs on a strict clock: blue made in its own cells, a rise that happens within minutes of sunset, a coat that refuses touch.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: original style that does not come from copying; a sense of timing others call luck; quiet, constant work (the all-day picking at copepods) that makes the public moment look easy; loyalty to one partner at a time.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: the Rooster's need to be seen and heard, wrapped in Pisces evasiveness. Admired from a distance, hard to hold; the bitter coat goes up before anyone has done harm. Can mistake perfect timing for the only acceptable timing and miss a night out of pride.
- **Love.** Our reading: Venus is exalted in Pisces and the Rooster keeps promises. Slow, exact courtship; chooses one partner and rises with them, side by side, on a shared signal. Wants a partner who shows up at the same place at the same hour without being asked twice.
- **Money.** Our reading: Rooster industry with a Pisces soft spot. Earns steadily through small, repeated effort; spends on beauty and craft; the risk is lending to a hard-luck story because the fish in you cannot say no and the rooster in you hates to look stingy.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: avoids the open fight and lets the coat do the refusing. When cornered, frank to the point of sting (the Rooster's bluntness). Do better by naming the problem at the first sign rather than going bitter.

## Careers (our reading)
- **Colour and surface work: textile dyeing, colour grading, ceramics glazes, cosmetics chemistry.** Our reading: the cyanophore bridge (blue made from the inside) plus the Rooster's craft standard. Tied to source m7/m9.
- **Live performance with a strict call time: stage management, broadcast, lighting design.** Our reading: the Rooster's time-keeping virtue and the sunset spawning window; China Highlights also lists broadcasting for Roosters.
- **Small-scale field science or aquaculture.** Our reading: patient, repeated observation in one habitat; the Palau study itself is the model.
- **Costume, uniform and ceremonial dress.** Our reading: the 官服魚 'official-robe fish' alias and the Rooster's comb as civility.
- **Counselling or chaplaincy with firm boundaries.** Our reading: Pisces compassion held inside a defined frame, the way the mucus coat lets the fish stay soft without being handled.

## Best matches
- **Whale Shark** (`whale-shark`): Pisces x Dragon. Dragon is the Rooster's six-harmony partner (secret friend), and both are Pisces. The vivid small one and the vast gentle one share a sea.
- **Slow Loris** (`slow-loris`): Scorpio x Snake. Snake is in the Rooster trine (Ox, Snake, Rooster), and Scorpio is a fellow Water sign. Both carry a defence you only meet up close.
- **Asian Elephant** (`asian-elephant`): Cancer x Ox. Ox completes the Rooster trine; Cancer is Water. Steady memory meets exact timing.
- **Shoebill** (`shoebill`): Capricorn x Ox. Ox trine, and Capricorn Earth is the classic complement to Pisces Water. A patient, still partner for a fish that moves on a clock.
- **Pangolin** (`pangolin`): Taurus x Snake. Snake trine; Taurus Earth complements Water. Armour beside slime: two animals that protect a soft body differently.

## Hard pairings
- **Penduline Tit** (`penduline-tit`): Virgo x Rabbit. Rabbit and Rooster are a traditional six-clash pair, and Virgo is Pisces's opposite sign, so both systems pull apart at once.
- **Moon Jellyfish** (`moon-jellyfish`): Pisces x Rabbit. Same Western sign, so it feels familiar, but the Rabbit-Rooster clash sits underneath.

## Candidate lines
1. Your blue was made in your own cells, and you can tell a borrowed colour from across the lagoon.
2. Twenty minutes either side of sunset is your whole window, and you would sooner wait for tomorrow's dusk than rise in the wrong light.
3. Someone admires you through the coral and reaches out, and the bitter coat does the refusing so your face never has to.
4. All day you pick at the reef, one copepod at a time, so the rise at dusk can look like it cost you nothing.
5. A court robe on a fish the size of a thumb: you carry rank lightly and see no reason to explain it.
6. When you choose, you rise one metre with one partner, side by side, on the same signal, and then let the current carry the rest.
