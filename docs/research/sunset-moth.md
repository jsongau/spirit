## The Sunset Moth as a symbol
- **Malagasy (Madagascar).** The word lolo means both butterfly and ghost, the spirit of a dead person. Malagasy belief, as reported by Wikipedia from Mannoni and the Bulletin de l'Academie malgache, holds that ancestors' souls can appear as moths or butterflies, so harming one is harming the ancestors. Strongest sourced item: the moth's only home treats its kind as a visiting ancestor. [s7] [s6]
- **Malagasy (local names for this moth).** Recorded local names adriandolo and lolonandriana join lolo (spirit, butterfly) with andriana (noble, king): 'noble spirit' or 'king butterfly'. A royal name for a Leo animal. Source chain is Wikipedia citing Decary 1950, Oberthur 1922, Webber 1855; I could not open the dictionary entry for andriana, so present as 'recorded as'. [s6]
- **Chinese (moth).** 飞蛾扑火 fēi é pū huǒ, the moth flies into the flame. First recorded in the Book of Liang (梁书 Liáng shū), where Emperor Gaozu used 如飞蛾之赴火 rú fēi é zhī fù huǒ to praise pursuit of a goal despite mortal risk; later readers turned it into a warning about fatal temptation. Fits Leo (Fire) acting on Metal: devotion and self-burning in one idiom. [s8]
- **Chinese (Rooster half).** 天鸡 tiān jī, the heavenly rooster on the giant Taodu peach tree (桃都 táo dū): when the first sun strikes the tree it crows and every rooster under heaven answers (Xuanzhongji 玄中记 Xuánzhōngjì, via Taiping Yulan). The Rooster as the voice that greets the Sun, Leo's ruling planet. [s10]
- **Chinese (Rooster half).** The rooster's five virtues in Han Ying's Hanshi Waizhuan (韩诗外传 Hánshī Wàizhuàn): the comb is civility (文 wén), spurs martial skill (武 wǔ), fighting the enemy courage (勇 yǒng), calling others to food benevolence (仁 rén), crowing on time trustworthiness (信 xìn). [s9]
- **Victorian Europe.** Its wings were made into jewellery in the Victorian era, and it is still collected and farmed for the butterfly trade (Wikipedia citing Carter 2000). Beauty that gets taken for the wrong thing and worn by others. [s6]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** Any 'sunset moth spirit animal / totem' meanings. Nothing traditional found beyond the Malagasy lolo belief; totem-blog content only. (no source)

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: fixed Fire, ruled by the Sun, meets yin Metal. Both halves face the dawn: Leo's planet is the Sun, and the Rooster is the bird that answers the first light on the Taodu tree. The Sunset Moth is a creature of noon in a family of night fliers, a 'noble spirit' in Malagasy naming. Fire controls Metal, so Leo's heat works the Rooster's precision into an edge rather than feeding it.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: presence that does not wait for permission, honesty and punctuality (the rooster's 信 xìn), color that changes with the viewer yet is real from every side, toxins of early life turned into protection.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: 飞蛾扑火. Flying at the bright thing and calling it devotion; Rooster pride that must correct the record; Leo touchiness when the display is misread. The live page's 'correcting instead of connecting' shadow is consistent with sourced Rooster traits (s4) and can stay.
- **Love.** Our reading: generous and demonstrative (Leo, s2), loyal and promise-keeping (Rooster, s4). Wants to be seen in full sun and described accurately. Needs a partner who praises the specific color, not the general glitter.
- **Money.** Our reading: Rooster resourcefulness with a Leo taste for display. Spends visibly, earns through being seen. The single-host-plant fact suggests one reliable source of income it will cross the whole island to follow.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: answers fast and in public, like a rooster at first light. Fire tempering Metal: heat first, then a precise, cutting sentence. Practice: crow once.
