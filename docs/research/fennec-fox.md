## The Fennec Fox as a symbol
- **Algeria (modern national symbol).** The fennec fox is the national animal of Algeria, and the Algeria national football team is nicknamed Les Fennecs. A small desert animal chosen to stand for a whole country's grit. [s8]
- **Arabic (name).** The word fennec comes from Arabic fanak, which Wikipedia says likely has Persian origins. Use only as a supporting detail, never as the hook (brief bans name trivia as the opener). [s8]
- **French literature (Saint-Exupery).** CAUTION. The popular claim that the fox in The Little Prince (1943) is a fennec is contested. English Wikipedia's Little Prince article says the fox is believed to be modelled on Saint-Exupery's New York friend Silvia Hamilton Reinhardt, or possibly his boxer dog Hannibal (NYT 1993, WSJ 2014). The fennec article only says the species is 'depicted in' the book. A blog says he followed fennec tracks to a den while stranded in the Libyan desert; that matches a known passage in Terre des hommes (Wind, Sand and Stars) but I could not open the primary text. Safe use: the fox's lesson, 'what is essential is invisible to the eye', and taming as the making of a bond, credited to the book, without claiming the fox is a fennec. [s10] [s8] [s11]
- **Chinese (fox in general, not the fennec).** 狐 hú, the fox, carries the 狐狸精 húli jīng tradition of shape-shifting fox spirits, good or bad by story. Guo Pu's commentary on the Shanhaijing calls the nine-tailed fox 九尾狐 jiǔwěihú an auspicious omen in times of peace. This is fox lore at genus level; the fennec itself does not live in China and has no Chinese folklore of its own. Use only as the meaning of the header glyph. [s12]
- **Saharan peoples (Tuareg, Amazigh).** Unsourced, do not use. Searches turned up only content-farm pages; no ethnographic source for fennec folklore located. (no source)
- **Spirit-animal blogs.** Unsourced, do not use. Generic 'fennec spirit animal means sensitivity/adaptability' claims have no traditional source. (no source)

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: mutable Air (Gemini) meets yin Wood (Rabbit). Gemini wants signal, talk, two channels open at once; the Rabbit wants the burrow and the counted exits. The Fennec is the night talker who does it underground: ears tuned to everything above, voice saved for the clan below. Mercury, the messenger, rules Gemini; the Rabbit is the guarded listener. Together: high intake, careful output.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: hears tone, not just words; lives lightly and adapts to scarce conditions; a sense of humor that keeps a group close; builds backup plans (several den entrances) without drama; deep loyalty to one partner and one small circle.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: the ears never come down. Gemini restlessness plus Rabbit guardedness can turn into constant scanning, half-presence, and a habit of running on scraps (the fox that never needs to drink) instead of asking for what it needs. Avoids hard conversations by keeping them light.
- **Love.** Our reading: playful and talkative early, then very private. Mates for keeps once the bond is set (ADW: fennecs mate for life). Needs a partner who joins the den rather than drags them out of it, and who reads chatter as affection.
- **Money.** Our reading: frugal by instinct, good at stretching small resources (water from roots and fruit). Keeps more than one income door open. Risk: underspending on itself until it runs dry.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: retreats underground first, deflects with wit. Pushed into a corner, it growls loud for its size. Repairs best in private, inside the family den, not in public.

## Careers (our reading)
- **Audio work: sound design, podcast production, music editing, acoustics.** Our reading: the ear is this animal's whole build (15 cm pinnae, enlarged bullae, s5).
- **Interpreting, translation, language teaching.** Our reading: Mercury-ruled Gemini plus Rabbit tact; catches tone as well as content.
- **User research, customer insight, intelligence analysis.** Our reading: picking up movement under the surface, like prey under sand.
- **Night-shift or remote roles: radio, nursing, overnight dispatch.** Our reading: nocturnal by nature (s5), steady in the quiet hours.
- **Small-team operations, logistics and contingency planning.** Our reading: the multi-entrance burrow; backups built before anyone asks.
- **Desert, dryland or water-conservation work.** Our reading: the waterless body; a literal fit with the animal's world.

## Hard pairings
- **Fork-tailed Drongo** (`fork-tailed-drongo`): Gemini x Rooster. Same sign, but Rabbit and Rooster are a traditional six-clash pair: easy banter, friction underneath.
- **Weaverbird** (`weaverbird`): Virgo x Rooster. Rabbit-Rooster clash, and Virgo Earth has no element harmony with Gemini Air (both Mercury-ruled, mutable, and in square).

## Candidate lines (draft, for the human editor)
1. Your den has three ways out, and only two people have ever been shown the third.
2. For seasons at a time you have lived on the moisture hidden in roots and fruit, and you call that being low maintenance.
3. Gemini gave you a night's worth of conversation and the Rabbit gave you a burrow to say it in, so your loudest hours happen underground, with your own clan.
4. Your feet are furred for sand that burns at noon and bites at midnight, which is how you cross moods other people have to wait out.
5. One mate, kept. Everything else in your life can shift like a dune, and that fixed point is why you can afford to let it.
6. Those enormous ears shed heat as much as they catch sound, so some of your listening is simply how you keep from overheating.
