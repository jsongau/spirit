# Koala dossier: Taurus x Year of the Rabbit

Slug `koala`. Element: Wood (from the Rabbit); Taurus adds Earth, fixed. Research date 2026-11-08. Everything marked 'Our reading' is interpretation, not tradition.

## Western half: Taurus
- Temperament: Grounded, steady, sensual, loyal, patient and dependable; slow to change, can turn obstinate, possessive or indulgent. Stabilizes and continues what Aries starts.
- Planet: Venus (the Moon is exalted in Taurus)
- Element: Earth (with Virgo and Capricorn)
- Modality: Fixed; opposite sign Scorpio
- Sources: [s1] [s2]

## Chinese half: Rabbit
- Temperament: Gentle, quiet, alert, considerate and conflict-averse; sharp observer, cautious and responsible. Shadow: indecisive, reluctant to show thoughts, can retreat from hard realities, sensitive to how others see them.
- Yinyang: Yin
- Element: Wood (fixed); earthly branch 卯 mǎo; Rabbit hour 5:00 to 7:00 (dawn)
- Trine: Rabbit, Goat, Pig
- Secret friend: Dog
- Clash: Rooster (Wikipedia also lists Dragon as a harm pair and Rat as a punishment pair)
- Lucky: Colors red, pink, purple, blue (both sources agree). Numbers 3 and 4 in both sources; China Highlights adds 6, Travel China Guide adds 9, so use only 3 and 4. Flowers: plantain lily (hosta) and jasmine per China Highlights.
- Sources: [s3] [s4] [s5]

## The Koala as a symbol
- **Gumbaynggirr (Mid North Coast NSW).** Dunggirr Gagu, the Koala Brothers: when rising seas cut the Ngambaa people off, the koala brothers used their long intestines as a bridge back to the mainland. The long gut as a lifeline is a direct mythic echo of the page's cecum fact. [s8]
- **Kullilla and Muruwari (told by Michael J Connolly); also in Mountford, The Dreamtime (1965).** Koobor the drought maker: a neglected boy learns to live on gum leaves, hoards the water and becomes the koala. Koalas are said in some stories to hold the rains, and disrespecting them brings drought. [s8] [s10]
- **Gumbaynggirr, Dunghutti, Ngambaa.** Yarriabini (Mount Yarrahapinni) is a koala Dreaming site; the name means 'koala rolling', from two giant koalas who fought and rolled down the mountain (Uncle Mark Flanders). [s8]
- **Birpai, Awabakal and others.** Koala as sacred totem: a relationship of protection, where the totem holder may not harm it and carries responsibility for it. [s8]
- **Dharug (Sydney).** The word koala comes from Dharug gula or gulamany, glossed as 'no drink'. Common Ground states the gloss without linguistic citation, so present it as 'often said to mean'. [s9]
- **Chinese (Rabbit half).** The Jade Rabbit keeps Chang'e company on the Moon and pounds the elixir of immortality; worshipped with the Moon since the Tang. White rabbits tied to long life (Baopuzi). Proverbs: 狡兔三窟 jiǎo tù sān kū, the crafty rabbit keeps three burrows; 守株待兔 shǒu zhū dài tù, the farmer who waits by the stump for another rabbit (Han Feizi). [s6] [s7]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** Generic 'koala spirit animal means relaxation/nurturing' claims found on totem blogs; no traditional source located. (no source)

Note: the First Nations stories come from a First Nations-led source (Common Ground). Before retelling them on a commercial page, credit the nation by name and keep the retelling short.

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: fixed Earth (Taurus) meets yin Wood (Rabbit). Taurus wants to settle on the one good thing; the Rabbit stays alert and keeps an exit. The Koala is the settled watcher: content in one place, yet quietly scanning. Both halves point at the Moon: the Moon is exalted in Taurus, and the Jade Rabbit lives on the Moon. Wood controls Earth, so the Rabbit's testing instinct keeps Taurus from settling unquestioned.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: discernment about quality others miss, patience that outlasts pressure, calm that lowers the temperature of a room, loyalty once trust is earned, a voice that lands hard because it is used rarely.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: 守株待兔. Waiting by the stump for the good thing to arrive again, mistaking caution for strategy, conflict avoidance (Rabbit) hardened by stubbornness (fixed Earth), hoarding energy or feelings the way Koobor hoarded water.
- **Love.** Our reading: Venus-ruled and yin. Slow to open, sensual and devoted once settled, needs a partner who reads stillness as care rather than distance. Fears being rushed and fears confrontation more than loss.
- **Money.** Our reading: Taurus earth plus Rabbit caution gives a saver who prefers known, steady returns over speculation; the risk is holding cash or a job too long out of comfort. Three burrows: keeps quiet backups.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: goes still and waits it out; rarely starts the fight and rarely forgets it. When pushed past the limit the soft one bites (兔子急了还咬人 tù zi jí le hái yǎo rén, per s6). Practice: say the small thing early.

- **Counselling, mediation, hospice or care work.** Our reading: Rabbit's gentle, conflict-lowering nature and Taurus steadiness; a calm presence others settle around.
- **Accounting, financial planning, conservative investing.** Our reading: the koala's energy budget as a money style; patient, risk-aware, three burrows.
- **Garden design, horticulture, botany, land care.** Our reading: Wood and Earth together; the koala's whole life is one tree.
- **Voice work, radio, singing, low-frequency presence.** Our reading: Venus rules the arts, and the koala's rare, outsized bellow (s11).
- **Research or specialist roles with long, uninterrupted focus.** Our reading: narrow and deep beats wide and shallow for this crossing.

- **Devil's Flower Mantis** (`devils-flower-mantis`): Scorpio x Rooster. Rabbit-Rooster is the traditional six-clash pair and Scorpio is Taurus's opposite sign. Both systems pull against each other here.

## Fact audit of the live page
- **OK (with softening)**: Koalas select trees with higher available nitrogen and lower sideroxylonal, even among trees of the same species; difference detectable only by taste and smell.
  Within Eucalyptus melliodora, feeding rate fell as sideroxylonal rose (s14). Visitation was lower than expected for low-nitrogen trees, but not higher for high-nitrogen ones; tree size was the strongest factor (s15). Suggest: 'fed less on trees richer in sideroxylonal and avoided nitrogen-poor ones'. The 'only by taste and smell' clause is UNVERIFIABLE; cut it. [s14] [s15]
- **WRONG (ratio)**: Cecum about two metres long, about four to five times body length, proportionally the largest of any mammal; food can take up to 100 hours to pass.
  Guinness: cecum 200 cm, koala total length 60 to 85 cm, so roughly 2.5 to 3 times body length, not 4 to 5. 'Proportionally largest' is supported (Guinness, 'for an animal'). The 100-hour passage figure is UNVERIFIABLE from what I could reach. [s12]
- **OK (range wide)**: Wild koalas rest or sleep 18 to 22 hours a day.
  PBS gives 18 to 20 hours sleeping. 22 is common online but I found no primary source; '18 to 20' or 'up to 20' is safer. [s13]
- **WRONG (forensic claim)**: Fingerprints close to human; forensic examiners reportedly had difficulty telling them apart; convergent evolution.
  The similarity and convergence are real (Henneberg et al., s17). The crime-scene confusion is unfounded: Snopes found no case, NSW police and Henneberg himself called it extremely unlikely (s16). Drop 'forensic examiners have reportedly had difficulty'. [s16] [s17]
- **WRONG (mechanism)**: Male bellow made with a permanently descended larynx (first in a marsupial); fundamental frequency about twenty times lower than expected, like an elephant.
  The 20x-lower, elephant-like pitch (about 27 Hz) comes from velar vocal folds, a separate organ above the larynx at the junction of nose and mouth, not from the larynx (Charlton et al. 2013, s11). The descended larynx (s18) lowers formants (resonances), which signal size; it does not set the pitch. Rewrite to credit the velar vocal folds. [s11] [s18]
- **OK**: Joey born tiny and undeveloped, grows in a backward-facing pouch, feeds on pap that delivers gut bacteria for eucalyptus.
  PBS: pouch opens toward the hind legs; pap supplies the gut microbes needed to digest eucalyptus. Suggest 'rear-opening' and note pap is a specialized form of maternal faeces (the page's 'distinct from ordinary waste' is fine but slightly coy). [s13]
