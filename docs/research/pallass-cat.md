## The Pallas's Cat as a symbol
- **Chinese (name).** The Chinese name is 兔猻 tù sūn (simplified 兔狲), so the Rabbit sits inside the animal's own name. The origin is disputed: one account says Turkic herders shouted a word for 'stop' that was written 兔狲; another ties 狲 to Mongolian loans (猞猁狲 for lynx) and reads 兔 as 'rabbit-sized' or 'the lynx that catches pikas' (鼠兔 shǔtù). Our China Story says plainly it has nothing to do with rabbits. Use as a sourced curiosity in the body, never as the hook. [p3] [p4] [p5] [p6]
- **Vietnamese zodiac.** Vietnam (and the Gurung zodiac) replaces the Rabbit with the Cat. A cat standing in the Rabbit's seat is a real, documented tradition, which gives a Rabbit-year cat honest footing. [p9]
- **Mongolian / Kyrgyz.** 'Manul' is the Mongolian name and 'manol' the Kyrgyz one. Wikipedia records ongoing domestic trade in skins and body parts for medicinal use in Mongolia. Recorded use, not a blessing: state it with its Near Threatened status in Mongolia and Endangered status in China. [p1] [p3]
- **Russian internet culture.** A 2008 Russian meme, 'Pet the cat', made the manul famous; Moscow Zoo took it as its mascot in 2012. Mongabay quotes Jan Janecka: the facial expression is 'almost like a meme'. Modern, but real and sourced. [p1] [p2]
- **Science of the record.** Moqanaki in Mongabay: 'This is a rare species. This is an elusive species. It's not easy to observe.' Researchers often get it as bycatch on snow leopard camera traps. CATnews 2010: population size and status largely unknown. The elusiveness itself is the symbol, and it is documented. [p2] [p3]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** No traditional folklore, Mongolian or Tibetan spirit-animal meaning, or proverb about the manul was found in any source we could open. Blog claims of a 'manul spirit animal' meaning are unsourced.

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: fixed Water (Scorpio) meets yin Wood (Rabbit). Water feeds Wood in the generating cycle, so the depth comes first and the form grows out of it. The manul is the watcher that is built rather than trained to be hidden: low ears, a coat that turns it into stone, a range so wide that meeting it is luck. Scorpio's privacy and the Rabbit's caution point the same way, toward cover.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: a read on people that arrives whole, patience in a cold place, the ability to look unbothered while working hard, loyalty to a very small circle, a presence felt over wide territory without constant visits.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: freezing flat against the rock long after the hawk has gone. Scorpio's walls and the Rabbit's retreat from hard realities stack, so withdrawal can pass for strategy. The face people read as grumpy may be telling them a story you never meant.
- **Love.** Our reading: slow to be found, then intensely loyal (Scorpio commits for the long haul; the Rabbit is loyal to those close). Needs a partner who does not take stillness as coldness, and who can tell the difference between a closed face and a closed heart.
- **Money.** Our reading: an ambush economy. Waits, watches, moves once. Comfortable with long dry spells, prefers borrowed shelter that has already proven itself over building from scratch. Risk: holding cash too long while the moment passes.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: flattens and goes quiet first, then answers with one precise strike. Scorpio holds grudges; the Rabbit avoids open fights. The repair is naming the problem out loud before the silence names it for you.

## Careers
- **Field research, wildlife camera-trap work, survey ecology.** Our reading: long patience in harsh places, and the manul itself is mostly known through camera traps.
- **Investigative research, audit, forensic accounting.** Our reading: Scorpio's appetite for what is hidden plus the Rabbit's care and responsibility.
- **Therapy, counselling, medicine.** Our reading: China Highlights lists medicine and health care among Rabbit careers; Scorpio's depth suits work behind closed doors.
- **Photography, documentary film.** Our reading: the craft of being unseen while seeing everything.
- **Security analysis, intelligence work.** Our reading: reads a field before anyone knows it is being read.
