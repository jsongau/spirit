# Bearded Vulture: research dossier

Capricorn x Year of the Dragon. Slug `bearded-vulture`. Researched 2026-11-08. Source ids in brackets point to the list at the end. Anything marked 'our reading' is interpretation, not tradition.

**Element:** Earth (Dragon's fixed element, branch 辰 chén). Capricorn is also Earth, so this is one of the doubled-Earth crossings.

## Western sign: Capricorn [s4] [s5]

- Temperament: Capricorn is read as the measured planner of the zodiac: loyal, hardworking, devoted, honest, built for mastery through long effort. The traditional shadow is pessimism, coldness, overwork, a harsh inner critic, held-back feeling and too much weight on status.
- Planet: Saturn, the planet of discipline and maturity
- Element: Earth (with Taurus and Virgo)
- Modality: Cardinal (starts a season, the winter solstice). Symbol is the Sea-Goat, mountain-goat hoof and fish tail. Classic opposition: Cancer.

## Chinese year: Dragon [s6] [s7] [s8]

- Temperament: Dragons are traditionally described as courageous, tenacious, intelligent, enthusiastic and confident, bold risk-takers who push against convention. Element sub-types color it: Earth Dragon (1988) is read as smart, ambitious and hardworking; Metal Dragon (2000) straightforward with a desire to be seen; Water Dragon (2012) persevering and farsighted; Wood Dragon (2024) more inward.
- Yin/yang: Yang
- Element: Earth (earthly branch 辰 chén). Note: the English Wikipedia Dragon (zodiac) page lists Wood, while its Chinese zodiac table lists Earth. Branch 辰 is an Earth branch; the site's Earth is the standard reading.
- Trine: Rat, Dragon, Monkey
- Secret friend: Rooster (六合 liù hé, six harmonies)
- Clash: Dog (六冲 liù chōng). Also a 'harm' pair (六害 liù hài) with Rabbit.
- Lucky colors and numbers: not included (per brief).

## The animal as a symbol

- **Persian / Iranian, and Mughal India.** The mythical Huma (Homa) is attributed to the bearded vulture, and Homa is a vernacular name for it. A bird of luck: if its shadow fell on you, you would rise to kingship. Killing one was said to bring death within forty days. Iranian and Zoroastrian tradition held vultures to be beneficial for cleaning carrion. Strongest symbol for a fortune-teller page: the bird whose shadow crowns. [s1] [s9]
- **Ancient Greek.** The ossifrage (Latin ossifragus, bone-breaker) was one of the few birds Greek soothsayers accepted as giving valid omens. Legend says the playwright Aeschylus was killed (456 or 455 BC) by a tortoise dropped by an 'eagle'; the bearded vulture is a likely candidate, since it drops tortoises as it drops bones. [s1]
- **Hebrew Bible.** Listed among birds forbidden as food (Leviticus 11:13). Its Hebrew name is peres, the 'breaker'; Shimon Peres took it as his surname. [s1]
- **Alpine German folk belief.** Lämmergeier means 'lamb-vulture', from the belief that it attacked lambs. A feared name for a bird that lives mostly on bone: a reputation the bird never earned. Useful as a 'misread' motif (our reading). [s1]
- **Tibetan Buddhist.** Wikipedia's bearded vulture article says that in Tibet corpses are fed to this vulture, with prayers and mantras, and the birds are blessed. Caution: the Sky burial article names Himalayan and griffon vultures as the main birds, with bones ground with tsampa for crows and hawks. Use only as 'vultures of the plateau are honored in sky burial', not as a bearded-vulture-specific rite. Sky burial is read as a last act of generosity and a teaching on impermanence. [s1] [s10]
- **Chinese.** Its Chinese name is 胡兀鹫 hú wù jiù; the Chinese Wikipedia article gives the nickname 骨头压碎机 gǔtou yāsuìjī, 'bone crusher', from the bone-dropping habit. This supports the page's 骨 gǔ (bone) header as a symbol. No traditional Chinese folklore about this species was found: unsourced, do not invent. [s11]

## The fusion (our reading)

- **Temperament:** Our reading: Capricorn's Saturn patience inside the Dragon's yang nerve, with Earth on both sides. The Dragon wants a big, bold life; Capricorn wants to earn it slowly. The bearded vulture settles the argument by choosing the hardest food on the mountain and the slowest way to eat it, then owning that niche outright. Read as someone ambitious in a quiet register: you want the summit, and you are willing to look like you came last.
- **Gifts:** Our reading: stamina for what other people give up on; the ability to make use of leftovers, the overlooked part of a deal, a job or a family story; strong sense of territory; a reputation that grows from rare skill; the Huma's aura, people feel luckier near you without knowing why.
- **Shadow:** Our reading: doubled Earth can harden. One proven method used past its use; Capricorn coldness plus Dragon pride can read as aloof; you can let others mistake you for a threat (the 'lamb-vulture' misread) rather than explain yourself; slow to forgive, slow to ask for help.
- **Love:** Our reading: late and deliberate. The real bird pairs slowly and holds several nests in one territory, rotating between them [s2]. In love you tend to test a person for a long time, then commit with total seriousness and build more than one kind of home together (routines, places, rituals). You give care whole and unsoftened (the parents bring food to the chick without regurgitating it [s1][s2]), which feels like honesty to some partners and bluntness to others. You need a partner who reads your quiet stretches as work, not distance.
- **Money:** Our reading: a value investor's temperament. You often make money where others see scraps: the unfashionable skill, the undervalued property, the second-hand thing with marrow left in it. Saturn keeps you saving; the Dragon tempts you toward one large, proud purchase. Risk: holding a losing position because the method worked once.
- **Conflict:** Our reading: you wait it out. Like the bird waiting at the cliff edge until the other scavengers finish [s2], you let the loud fight burn off, then come in with the one point that matters. The risk is that waiting reads as indifference, and that when you finally drop the bone, it lands hard.

## Careers

- **Restoration, conservation, salvage, archival work.** Making value from what others discarded: the bone after the meat is gone.
- **Forensics, pathology, audit, investigative journalism.** Comfort with what other people avoid looking at, and patience to take it apart. China Highlights also lists journalist and lawyer for Dragons [s8].
- **Engineering, architecture, structural work.** Capricorn's long build plus the Dragon's scale; China Highlights lists engineer and architect for Dragons [s8].
- **Turnaround leadership, distressed-asset investing.** Taking on the problem no one else wants and owning it outright.
- **Mountain guiding, wildlife or field science, hospice and end-of-life care.** Altitude, solitude, and the vulture's old role as the honored cleaner at the end of life (our reading of the Persian and Tibetan threads).

## Best matches [s6] [s16]

- **Andean Condor** (`andean-condor`): Capricorn x Rooster. Rooster is the Dragon's secret friend (六合), and the same Earth sign. Two mountain vultures: the closest kin on the wheel.
- **Superb Bird of Paradise** (`superb-bird-of-paradise`): Taurus x Rooster. Secret friend plus Earth with Earth. Their display draws out the side of you that wants to be seen; your steadiness grounds their show.
- **New Caledonian Crow** (`new-caledonian-crow`): Virgo x Monkey. Monkey shares the Dragon's trine, and Virgo is a fellow Earth sign. Two problem-solvers who use the world as a tool (the crow's sticks, the vulture's rock).
- **Marbled Polecat** (`marbled-polecat`): Capricorn x Rat. Rat shares the Dragon's trine, same Earth sign. Note: the live page lists this animal under 'temperaments that stretch yours', which runs against the trine tradition.

## Hard pairings

- **Giant Otter** (`giant-otter`): Aries x Dog. Dog is the Dragon's clash (六冲), and Aries squares Capricorn (cardinal Fire against cardinal Earth). A loud, fast, group-driven nature against your slow solitude.
- **Tibetan Blue Bear** (`tibetan-blue-bear`): Capricorn x Dog. Same sign and same mountains, but the Dog clashes with the Dragon. Two people who understand each other's work and still argue over who holds the ridge.

6. When you finally give your heart, you give it whole, with nothing chewed down to make it easier to swallow.
