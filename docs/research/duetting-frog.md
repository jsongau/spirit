## The Duetting Frog as a symbol
- **The species itself (biology as symbol).** The page's sources are all about Xenopus laevis, the African clawed frog. In murky night ponds a receptive female answers a calling male with a rapid clicking call called rapping; the male answers with a changed trill and swims to her. An unreceptive female gives a slow ticking that silences him. Male-female duetting is very rare in amphibians. This is the only true basis for the name 'Duetting Frog', which is not a recognized common name. [s6] [s7] [s8]
- **Modern medicine (1930s to 1960s).** Lancelot Hogben, working from South African clawed frogs, built the frog pregnancy test: a female injected with a pregnant woman's urine releases eggs, in as little as five hours. It was the standard test for about 15 years until chemical assays replaced it in the 1960s. The frog literally answered a question for millions of people. [s10]
- **Ancient Egypt.** Heqet, a frog-form goddess of fertility, linked to the final stages of childbirth and called 'She who hastens the birth'; frog amulets were probably worn in childbirth. Pairs strikingly with the Xenopus pregnancy test, but it is a general frog symbol, not this species. [s11]
- **Classical Chinese (Zhuangzi, ch. 17 Autumn Floods).** The frog of the dilapidated well, 埳井之鼃 kǎn jǐng zhī wā, boasts to the turtle of the Eastern Sea that its well is the whole world, until the turtle describes the sea. Source of the idiom 井底之蛙 jǐng dǐ zhī wā, the frog at the bottom of the well. Fits this crossing's shadow: judging the whole pond from one silence. [s12]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** Frog spirit-animal meanings (cleansing, rebirth, abundance) and the claim that 蛙 wā puns on 娃 wá (baby) in Chinese fertility lore; found only on blogs, no reliable source opened. No Indigenous southern African tradition specific to Xenopus was found. 

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Air (Libra) starts the exchange and weighs the reply; yang Earth (the Dog) guards what the reply builds. The Duetting Frog sends the first honest call into dark water and then commits only to what answers it correctly. Both halves value fairness and loyalty, so the exchange itself is the vow.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: telling a true answer from noise, honesty that invites honesty back, steady loyalty once a bond is confirmed, a gift for adjusting your next move to what the other person actually said.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: the frog in the well. One slow reply gets read as the whole pond going silent. Libra's conflict avoidance plus the Dog's difficulty naming feelings can turn into withdrawing without a word.
- **Love.** Our reading: slow to fall (Dog), courteous and partnership-minded (Libra, Venus). Needs a partner who answers, out loud and specifically. Once answered, faithful for the long run. A clear 'no' is respected; silence is the hard thing.
- **Money.** Our reading: Earth caution, Libra balance. Saves steadily, dislikes debt, wants agreements in writing. Risk: freezing a good decision while waiting for confirmation that never comes.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: prefers one honest statement to a fight, then waits. Will not chase. Growth is naming the silence out loud before going silent in return.
