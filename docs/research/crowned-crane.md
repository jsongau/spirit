## The Crowned Crane as a symbol
- **Baganda (Uganda) and the Ugandan state.** The grey crowned crane is Uganda's national bird and stands on its flag (adopted 9 October 1962) and coat of arms. Among the Baganda it is a symbol of good fortune, longevity and wealth. The British had first chosen it for the colonial badge to avoid favoring one region. [s9] [s5] [s6]
- **Rwanda (Banyiginya and other clans).** A totem of some of Rwanda's largest clans, associated with peace and long life; for the Banyiginya clan, harming one was a serious offense. The same prestige drove a habit of keeping clipped cranes at home, which harmed wild numbers. Tell it with the conservation note. [s10] [s8]
- **East African folktale ('Arap Sang and the Cranes').** Cranes carry a lost king home across the land; he rewards them with golden crowns; people hunt them for the gold, so the king turns the gold into the straw-like feathers they wear today. The ICF does not name the people of origin, so do not assign it to a nation. [s11]
- **African farmers (as reported by Kaohsiung Zoo).** The bird calls softly at dawn, noon and midnight, and farmers are said to have used the calls as a clock. Single source, a government zoo; use with attribution or not at all. [s12]
- **Nigeria.** The black crowned crane, the other Balearica species, is Nigeria's national bird. Genus-level item; name the species honestly. [s6]
- **Unsourced or conflated, do not use.** Chinese 鶴 hè longevity symbolism (仙鶴 xiān hè, cranes with immortals) belongs to the red-crowned crane 丹頂鶴 dāndǐng hè of East Asia, not this African bird; using it here would be a species borrow. Generic 'crane spirit animal' meanings come only from blogs. Zulu and Xhosa crane lore found online mostly concerns the blue crane. 

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Air (Libra) initiates and arranges; yin Wood (the Rabbit) keeps it gentle and avoids the hard edge. The Crowned Crane makes ceremony out of ordinary days and keeps the peace with style. Both halves dislike conflict, so the work of this crossing is saying the plain thing.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: grace that makes others feel considered, reading a group before it speaks, loyalty to one partner and one place, taste, a calm that holds a flock together.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: the dance as a cover. Libra's people-pleasing and the Rabbit's retreat from hard realities can leave the true sentence unsaid for years. Image management can outrun honesty.
- **Love.** Our reading: Venus-ruled, courteous, romantic in gesture; shows love by making moments beautiful. Monogamous by temperament, chooses once and builds around it. Needs a partner who asks for the plain version too.
- **Money.** Our reading: spends on beauty and hosting, and on keeping the peace. Wood's growth and Rabbit caution help it save once the reason is clear. Risk: paying to avoid an awkward conversation.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: smooths before it breaks; would rather redecorate than argue. Growth is the deep boom: one clear statement, then the dance.
