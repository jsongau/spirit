## The Paradise Tanager as a symbol
- **Andean and coastal Peru (pre-Columbian featherwork).** The Met's Radiance from the Rain Forest exhibition names 'the spectacular Paradise Tanager' as one of the small birds whose feathers were set in mosaic on crowns and headbands; feathers dressed people of high rank, and the Inka laid feathered cloth for royal weddings. The bird's color was worn by the chosen. Strongest, most specific item. [s9]
- **Spanish-speaking Amazonia.** Its common Spanish name is siete colores, 'seven colors' (Britannica). A bird known by its range of colors rather than any one of them. [s2]
- **Tupi (Brazil).** 'Tanager' and the genus Tangara come from Tupi tangara, a bird name of uncertain meaning (via Portuguese and Modern Latin). The common 'means dancer' gloss is UNSOURCED; do not use. [s10]
- **Chinese (name and idiom).** Chinese name 仙靓唐纳雀 xiān liàng tángnàquè, roughly 'fairy-beautiful tanager' (our gloss of 仙 xiān and 靓 liàng). The idiom 雀屏中目 què píng zhòng mù (also 雀屏中选 què píng zhòng xuǎn) means 'to be chosen as son-in-law', from the story of a peacock painted on a screen. Here 雀 què is the peacock, not the tanager: use it only as a Libra-partnership echo of the header character, clearly labelled. [s11] [s12]
- **Greek (Libra's ruler, Venus).** In Sappho's Ode to Aphrodite, the goddess arrives in a chariot 'yoked with lovely sparrows'. Venus rules Libra; small bright birds carry love. A bridge only; not about tanagers. [s13] [s14]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** No Indigenous Amazonian myth specifically about the paradise tanager was found. Totem-blog 'tanager spirit animal' meanings (joy, self-expression) have no traditional source. (no source)

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Air (Libra) meets yin Fire (Snake). Libra moves toward people and harmony; the Snake keeps its own counsel. The Paradise Tanager is the social bird with a private core: at ease in any mixed company, always the most recognizable in it, never handing over everything. Air feeds Fire, so company is what lights this one up.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: grace in mixed groups, a style people remember, fairness that weighs every side, careful speech that lands, an eye for beauty and quality (Venus plus the Snake's taste for the best).
- **Shadow.** Our reading: spreading itself across many flocks so no one holds all of it; Libra indecision meeting Snake secrecy; choosing the brilliant surface and hiding the dark layer that makes it shine; adding one more flourish to a thing that was already finished (画蛇添足 huà shé tiān zú).
- **Love.** Our reading: Venus-ruled and relationship-minded, yet yin and private. Courts with charm and polish, wants to be chosen, then tests slowly whether it is safe to show the dark under-layer. Best with someone who likes company as much as it does but asks for the quiet hour too.
- **Money.** Our reading: Snake taste for owning the best (s17) plus Libra's love of beautiful things: spends on quality and appearance. Gift: careful judgment once it slows down. Risk: buying the plumage.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: Libra avoids the fight, the Snake remembers it. Keeps the flock calm on the surface and decides privately. Practice: name the preference while the flock is still deciding which tree to move to.
