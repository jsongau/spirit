## The Mountain Pygmy Possum as a symbol
- **Australian natural history (modern lore).** Known to science first from a fossil jaw in a small limestone block from NSW cave deposits (described 1890s, thought extinct). In August 1966 skiers found a 'strange looking rat' in their ski hut on Mt Hotham; the animal, named George, was studied by Norman Wakefield and the species was recognised as alive. Parks Victoria titles its account 'Back from the dead and back again?' [s11] [s6] [s7]
- **Mt Hotham, Victoria (conservation lore).** The 'tunnel of love': a rock-filled corridor under the Great Alpine Road, first built in the 1980s at Mt Higginbotham, lets males reach the higher-altitude females. Genetic work found the road was a major barrier to breeding; a second tunnel followed around 2018. [s12] [s6]
- **GunaiKurnai (Krauatungalung clan) and other south-eastern nations; the moth, not the possum.** The Bogong moth that feeds the possum each spring was also a human feast: ground moth remains 1,600 to 2,100 years old on a grindstone in Cloggs Cave, and 1830s to 1850s accounts of Aboriginal gatherings in the Alps during the migration. Use only as shared ground (the possum and people waited for the same moth), never as possum totem lore. [s15]
- **Chinese (Rabbit half).** 狡兔三窟 jiǎo tù sān kū, 'the crafty rabbit keeps three burrows', from Feng Xuan's advice in the Zhanguo Ce (戰國策 Zhànguó Cè). It now describes keeping several escape routes, with a slightly derogatory colour. It fits the possum's real habit of changing hibernation crevices up to 12 times a winter (s8). [s5] [s8]
- **Chinese (proverb on the page).** 守株待兔 shǒu zhū dài tù, the farmer waiting by the stump. The page turns it around (the possum waits only after stocking the den). Honest use, labelled as its own reading. [s5]
- **Name.** Burramys parvus is translated by ADW as 'small rock mouse' (s7). Usable as a gloss, not as a hook. [s7]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** No Aboriginal story specific to Burramys was found. Possum-skin cloaks and possum totems in the literature concern brushtail and ringtail possums, other families; do not borrow them. Generic 'pygmy possum spirit animal' blog meanings: no source. 

## The fusion
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Earth (Capricorn) under yin Wood (Rabbit). Saturn's long timeline and the Rabbit's caution meet in an animal that stocks a rock crevice in autumn and sleeps under snow until the melt. Wood controls Earth in the five phases; here the Rabbit's restlessness keeps Capricorn from trusting one structure blindly, which matches the real possum walking out mid-winter to change dens.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: foresight, provisioning, exactness about what will hold, endurance through long lean stretches, loyalty to a small circle on home ground.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: the recount. Capricorn guardedness plus Rabbit reluctance to show the mind means re-testing proven ground, hoarding reserves as worry, and hiding plans from the people who share the den.
- **Love.** Our reading: slow to commit (Capricorn wants long-term stability, s2; the Rabbit is faithful, s4). Will cross real obstacles for the one (the tunnel). Needs a partner who does not take the long winter silences personally.
- **Money.** Our reading: a natural cacher. Builds reserves early and stores them where no one else looks; risk is under-spending a surplus that was meant to be lived on.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: avoids open fights, relocates quietly instead. When cornered the soft one bites (兔子急了還咬人 tùzi jí le hái yǎo rén, s5). Practice: name the problem before moving dens.

## Careers
- **Logistics, supply planning, emergency and disaster preparedness.** Our reading: caching for a season you cannot watch; Capricorn's planning.
- **Accounting, actuarial work, conservative finance.** Our reading: Capricorn's skill with money (s2) and the Rabbit's caution.
- **Alpine ecology, conservation, park ranger or seed-bank work.** Our reading: the animal's own world; Rabbit's traditional fit with cultivation and breeding (s4).
- **Building surveying, structural inspection, quality assurance.** Our reading: the instinct to test whether a gap will hold before trusting it.
- **Health care, medicine, education.** Our reading: China Highlights lists these for the Rabbit (s4); Capricorn's responsibility makes them a long-haul career.

## Best matches
- **Clark's Nutcracker** (`clarks-nutcracker`): Virgo x Goat. Goat is in the Rabbit trine; Virgo shares Capricorn's Earth. Another mountain seed-cacher: two animals that survive winter on what they stored in autumn.
- **Tibetan Blue Bear** (`tibetan-blue-bear`): Capricorn x Dog. Same sign, and Dog is the Rabbit's secret friend (six harmonies). Two high-country winter sleepers.
- **Snow Leopard** (`snow-leopard`): Scorpio x Goat. Rabbit trine; Scorpio Water complements Capricorn Earth. Both live in boulder country above the treeline.
- **Bongo** (`bongo`): Taurus x Pig. Pig is in the Rabbit trine; Taurus is Earth like Capricorn. Already on the live page.
- **Star-nosed Mole** (`star-nosed-mole`): Virgo x Pig. Rabbit trine plus Earth sign. Precision workers in cramped dark spaces.

## Hard matches
- **Andean Condor** (`andean-condor`): Capricorn x Rooster. Same sign, but Rabbit and Rooster are the traditional six-clash pair. Both mountain animals; one soars in the open, one hides in the cracks.
- **Emperor Penguin** (`emperor-penguin`): Cancer x Rooster. Rabbit-Rooster clash, and Cancer is Capricorn's opposite sign.

## Candidate lines
1. For seventy years your kind was a jawbone in a block of limestone, written off. Then skiers found one alive in their hut on Mt Hotham. Being counted out early has never worried you much.
2. In the middle of winter you wake, walk out across the snow-covered rocks, find a crack two boulders over that holds a steadier cold, and go back to sleep. The Rabbit in you calls it the third burrow.
3. Every autumn you stock a crevice with plum-pine seed you will barely be awake to eat, and you hide it where only you would think to look.
4. You go under in April, months before the first real snow, because your body reads the turn of the season sooner than the mountain admits it.
5. Your whole spring hangs on a moth flying up from the plains, hundreds of kilometres below. You cannot hurry it. You just make sure the den is ready the night it lands.
6. Two degrees above freezing, under a roof of snow, heart barely moving: this is the one stretch when the Capricorn in you rests, because the count was finished in autumn.
