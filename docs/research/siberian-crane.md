## The Siberian Crane as a symbol
- **Sakha (Yakut) and Yukaghir, northeast Siberia.** The white crane is sacred, tied to the sun, to spring and to kind celestial spirits; in Yakut epics shamans take the form of white cranes. The CMS booklet adds that some Yakutians believe seeing a Siberian crane can heal a sick person. [s6] [s9]
- **Khanty, western Siberia.** After killing a dangerous bear, Khanty people dance in crane costume to drive off the bear's spirit, and they keep reindeer away from crane nesting marshes (CMS Siberian Crane booklet). The crane as the one who sees a dangerous spirit out. [s9]
- **China (cranes in general).** Cranes carried the immortals and stood for long life; the white feathers recalled white hair, and a pair could mean an old married couple. Shoulao, the god of longevity, rides a crane (Met essay). The Met speaks of cranes generally, not this species; its own name is 白鶴 báihè, 'white crane', and the Siberian crane is the white crane of Poyang Lake. [s10] [s11]
- **India.** Known as the 'Lily of Birds' (CMS booklet). A 17th-century court artist painted it, and it wintered at Bharatpur until the last bird was seen in 2002 (Wikipedia). A story of a route that was lost. [s9] [s6]
- **Iran.** A single bird named Omid has wintered in Iran since 2006 to 2007, the last of the western flock (Wikipedia). The meaning of the name was not given in the source I opened; do not gloss it without a source. [s6]
- **Chinese, Horse half (the page's proverb).** 老馬識途 lǎo mǎ shí tú, 'the old horse knows the way': from the Han Feizi, where Guan Zhong, lost with Duke Huan of Qi's army, freed the old horses and followed them back to the road. Verified; the page tells it correctly. [s12]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** Russian 'cranes as fallen soldiers' (ADW mentions songs) concerns cranes in general, likely the common crane; not specific to this species. Generic 'crane spirit animal' blog meanings are unsourced. [s5]

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: cardinal Earth (Capricorn) meets yang Fire (Horse). Fire generates Earth, so the Horse's heat and appetite for distance feed Capricorn's long plan. The Horse wants to be gone; Capricorn wants to arrive somewhere that holds. The Siberian Crane does both: one of the longest crane migrations, aimed at one lake.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: stamina over very long distances, exact timing, loyalty to a few places and people, the patience to refuel before the next leg, the ability to carry a route in memory.
- **Shadow.** Our reading: gone for half the year. Holds ground even at rest, rations its presence, and treats arrival as something that must be perfect before it can happen.
- **Love.** Our reading: Horse warmth with Capricorn seriousness. Monogamous in spirit (the species pairs and duets), shows love by coming back on schedule. Needs a partner who does not read the long absences as indifference.
- **Money.** Our reading: Capricorn saving with Horse risk. Builds steady reserves like a crane fattening at a stopover, then spends big on one bold move or one trip.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: leaves the ground rather than fights on it, then returns with a settled view. The Horse half can flare once, hard. Practice: say the half-formed view before the flight.

- **Logistics, supply chain, aviation operations, long-haul planning.** Our reading: a mind for routes, timing and staging points; China Highlights lists pilot and tour operator for the Horse.
- **Field science, conservation, wetland or water management.** Our reading: the crane's whole life depends on one lake's water level; Capricorn patience for long projects.
- **Foreign correspondent, translator, diplomat.** Our reading: Horse communication skills (China Highlights lists journalist and translator) plus Capricorn reliability across borders.
- **Project or programme leadership.** Our reading: Capricorn's discipline and the Horse's taste for leading and deciding.
- **Seasonal or rotational work: expedition guiding, offshore, polar stations.** Our reading: built to live in two places and keep faith with both.

## Fact audit of the live page
- **WRONG (too generic)**: Header hanzi 鹤 (rail__han and serial-han)
  鹤 hè (traditional 鶴) means crane in general, so it is not false, but it is not this bird's name. The Siberian crane is 白鶴 báihè, 'white crane' (simplified 白鹤). Use 白鶴 báihè with pinyin. [s11]
- **OK**: Fact 01: bare brick-red skin on forecrown, face and sides of head, on both sexes, absent in young birds, which keep feathers and cinnamon plumage
  ADW: brick-red featherless cap on forecrown, face and side of head; young lack it and are cinnamon. Wikipedia calls juveniles 'dingy brown'; both acceptable. [s5] [s6]
- **OK**: Fact 02: serrated bill helps grip slippery prey and pull up roots and tubers
  ADW: serrated bill aids catching slippery prey and feeding on underground roots. [s5]
- **OK (soften)**: Fact 03: unison call; male raises both wings fully overhead, female calls in time with wings folded at her sides
  ADW: females keep wings at their sides and folded rather than lifting them over the back as males do. Say 'lifts his wings over his back' rather than 'fully overhead'. [s5]
- **UNVERIFIABLE (partly OK)**: Fact 04: territorial in breeding season and winter, unusual among cranes; average territory about 625 km2; roosts apart from other crane species
  Territorial in both seasons: OK (ADW; Wikipedia says feeding territories in winter, sometimes small loose flocks). 625 km2 appears only in ADW and is implausible as a defended territory (25 by 25 km); no second source. 'Unusual among cranes' and 'roosts apart from other species' found in no source. Cut the number and both clauses. [s5] [s6]
- **OK (soften number)**: Fact 05: eastern population migrates roughly 5,000 km from Yakutia to Poyang Lake, staging at Momoge and Zhalong for a month or two
  Yakutia breeding and Poyang wintering: Wikipedia. Zhalong and Momoge as stopovers: ICF. Over 80% of the world population used Momoge for about 1.5 months: CLP. ICF gives the East Asian flyway as nearly 6,000 km; I found no source for 5,000. Say 'about 5,000 to 6,000 km' or cite ICF. [s6] [s7] [s8]
- **OK (soften)**: Fact 06: more than 95 percent of the entire wild population winters at Poyang Lake
  Wikipedia gives both about 93% and 98%; ICF says 'virtually all the East Asian population'. Population counts are rising (Mongabay 2025: near 7,000 in recent counts). Say 'nearly the whole wild population'. [s6] [s7] [s13]
- **OK / UNVERIFIABLE**: Intro: one of the longest migrations of any crane; critically endangered; pairs with one mate for life; defends the same wetland in two countries
  Longest migrations and Critically Endangered (the only crane in that category): Wikipedia. Monogamous: ADW. 'For life' and 'the same wetland' each year are not stated in any source I opened; soften to 'pairs and duets with one mate'. [s5] [s6]
- **OK**: Proverb: 老马识途, Duke Huan's army lost, followed the old horses home
  Han Feizi, Shuo Lin I: Guan Zhong freed the old horses and followed them back to the road. Page labels the Horse pairing as its own reading. [s12]
- **OK**: Lunar New Year note: January or early February birthdays can belong to the Snake
  Capricorn runs to about 20 January, so the note applies; the previous year before Horse is Snake. [s1] [s3]
