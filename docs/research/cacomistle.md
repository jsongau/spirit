## Chinese year animal
- Temperament: Diligent, compassionate, generous, calm under pressure, responsible, tolerant and trustworthy. Shadow: trusting to the point of being fooled, rarely asks for help, impatient, not good at managing money. The pig symbolizes wealth, honesty and plenty (piggy banks).
- Yinyang: Yin

## The animal as a symbol
- **Nahuatl (Aztec Mexico).** The name comes from Nahuatl tlahcomiztli, from tlahco- 'half, middle' + miztli 'cougar', so 'half-puma' or 'half-cat'. Miztli itself is attested in Sahagun and Molina's 1571 dictionary. A name that sizes the animal as half of a big cat is the one genuine cultural hook. [s7, s8, s6]
- **Mexico, prehispanic myth (negative finding).** Chilango, citing UNAM experts, says the cacomixtle does not appear in prehispanic mythology, though people lived alongside it. Treat this honestly: there is no myth to borrow. The page should not invent one. [s9]
- **Greek (Gemini half).** Castor and Pollux: Pollux asked Zeus to share his immortality with his dead twin, and the two were united in the heavens. Pairs well with an animal whose life runs on calls traded with a neighbor it rarely meets. [s1]
- **China, proverb (Pig half).** 人怕出名猪怕壮 rén pà chū míng zhū pà zhuàng: people fear fame the way pigs fear fattening up for slaughter; fame has its price. Already on the live page (in traditional characters 豬 zhū, 壯 zhuàng). [s14]
- **Other species, do not use here.** 'Miner's cat' (settlers kept ringtails as mousers), Spanish 'nimble thief' gloss, and the Mexico City urban cacomixtles seen in all 16 boroughs all refer to Bassariscus astutus, the ringtail, which is a separate Zodi Animal (Taurus x Monkey, slug ringtail). ADW gives the 'nimble thief' meaning without a source. [s10, s11]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** Any 'cacomistle spirit animal' or totem meaning: none found with a traditional source.

## The fusion (our reading)
- **Temperament.** Our reading: mutable Air (Gemini) over yin Water (Pig). Gemini talks, the Pig forages alone and does not perform. The Cacomistle is the voice in the canopy: solitary, high, unseen, and calling to its neighbors for hours. Mercury gives the calls; the Pig gives the patient night of feeding on figs. Air sits outside the five phases, so the Pig's Water is the only true element; Gemini colors it without changing it.
- **Gifts.** Our reading: long-distance closeness (the hours-long call); range without fuss; generosity and trust from the Pig; quick reading of a situation from above; comfort being half of two things (half-puma, raccoon kin, twin).
- **Shadow.** Our reading: Gemini's inconsistency plus the Pig's reluctance to ask for help. Talking instead of landing; staying a rumor; letting a neighbor know your voice but never your face. The fattened-pig proverb used as an excuse never to be seen.
- **Love.** Our reading: loves by keeping in touch, daily, across distance; a long-distance relationship is less scary to it than a shared floor. Twin logic: once committed, will share its best with the partner the way Pollux shared his sky.
- **Money.** Our reading: the Pig's luck with plenty and weak money management, plus Gemini's many side interests. Earns from several branches at once; needs one dull, fixed account it does not touch.
- **Conflict.** Our reading: moves rather than fights; changes branches, keeps talking, avoids the face-to-face. Territories do not overlap, so it keeps clear borders and defends them by voice, not claws. Helpful habit: agree on one fixed place to settle things.

## Other page issues
- Name collision: Dictionary.com's first sense of 'cacomistle' is Bassariscus astutus, the ringtail, and Wikipedia notes the name is used for both. The GRID has both Ringtail (Taurus x Monkey) and Cacomistle (Gemini x Pig). The page should say plainly it means B. sumichrasti, the Central American cacomistle, and keep ringtail facts (foot rotation, miner's cat, Mexico City sightings) on the ringtail page.
- Match I and II reasons say 'Your Water feeds their Wood: you are the warmth in it'. Warmth is the Wood-to-Fire image; Water feeding Wood should read as rain or roots.
- Page lists ADW Bassariscus astutus as a source on the cacomistle page: that is where the borrowed foot-rotation fact came from.
- Lines are set in 'a room' ('the moment a room starts taking attendance'); brief wants lines inside the canopy.
- No January/February lunar new year note: correct for Gemini.
