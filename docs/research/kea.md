## The Kea as a symbol

- **Māori (name).** The name kea copies the bird's own call (written 'keee aaa'). A bird that announces itself by name: a good image for a Gemini voice. [s1] [s3]
- **Māori, Waitaha.** The Waitaha people held the kea, with the kāhu (harrier) and ruru (morepork), to be kaitiaki, guardians of their people. The trickster is also a protector. [s1]
- **New Zealand high-country lore.** Hut stories of kea sliding down roofs before dawn, riding a knocked-over ski downhill, stealing nails and hiding them sorted by size, and a snow-dropping prank at one hut that later turned up at Arthur's Pass, 30 km away. Mischief that gets taught and passed on. [s2]
- **Colonial New Zealand.** High-country sheep farming from the 1870s brought accusations against kea, a bounty, and an estimated 150,000 birds shot before full legal protection in 1986. The bird blamed for more than it did: a strong shadow image. [s2]
- **Modern New Zealand.** Voted New Zealand's Bird of the Year in 2017 (7,311 votes), 'known fondly for its cheekiness.' Loved for exactly the trait that once got it shot. [s6]
- **Chinese (Monkey half, not the kea).** Sun Wukong of Journey to the West: stone-born, intelligent, 'naughty and impish,' who rebels when given a lowly post and causes the Havoc in Heaven. The cultural model for the clever, rule-testing Monkey. [s12]
- **Unsourced, do not use.** 'Clown of the mountains' / 'clown of the Southern Alps' nickname appears in search titles only; not verified on a reliable page. Generic 'kea spirit animal' meanings found online have no traditional basis. (no source)

## The fusion (ours)

**Temperament.** Ours: Gemini's Air wants to know; Monkey Metal wants to handle. The Kea is the mind that learns through its hands. You tend to understand a thing once you have taken it apart, and you narrate what you find while you are still finding it. Both halves are yang and both are restless, so the Kea runs warm and fast, and boredom is its real enemy.

**Gifts.** Ours: reading an unfamiliar system quickly; making hard problems feel like play (anchored by the play call that makes other kea play); teaching by showing, the way the snow-drop prank spread between huts; staying cheerful in thin, cold places where others give up.

**Shadow.** Ours: touching what was fine, testing people the way you test latches, getting blamed for damage you only meant as interest (the bounty years are the image). Gemini's inconstancy plus the Monkey's opportunism can read as unreliable.

**Love.** Ours: falls for the person who stays interesting. Courts with jokes and questions, needs a partner who enjoys being figured out and pokes back. Trust grows when the other person lets the Kea in on the workings of their life. Loses interest when everything is already known; does best with someone who keeps a little mystery and a lot of humor.

**Money.** Ours, with the traditional Monkey link to trading and opportunism (s9): quick to spot a deal and quick to earn, also quick to spend on the next gadget or trip. Does well with a second person or an automatic rule holding the savings.

**Conflict.** Ours: defuses with a joke first, argues with facts second, and can outtalk people without meaning to. Under real pressure the Kea flies off to another ridge rather than sitting in the cold. Growth is staying in the room long enough to finish the repair.
